The program is designed to create a pedigree of animals.
Keep information about the animal: name, gender, father, mother, date of birth, date of death, place of birth, place of death, name, note, child, son, daughter, brother, sister, wife, husband, wife, wedding date, wedding place

Program functions:

  • Making a list of animals;
  • Construction of the animal's pedigree;
  • Construction of a pedigree branch of an animal;
  • Compiling a list of generations;
  • Compiling a general and personal photo album;
  • Compiling a general and personal video archive;
  • Saving data in an archive.
