It is necessary to study your family history.
Studying the past can tell parents what inclinations and talents their children may have, how to raise and develop them.
Guardian angels are believed to be the ancestors that we know about.
With the birth of a child, the family becomes richer the whole world.
The study of genealogy is akin to historical investigation.
You can start small.
The program is designed to build an animated 3D family tree.
When the program starts, the tree rotates clockwise.
Controlling tree rotation is simple:
- when you click on the screen, the rotation of the tree stops.
- when you move your finger across the screen from left to right or from right to left, the tree begins to rotate in the corresponding direction.
The tree is formed in a random manner and consists of a trunk, and large and small branches.
Each person is on his own node, from which he can be transferred to another free node.
The program is menu controlled.
Insert menu - used to insert a new person’s icon into the tree from the photo gallery on the device.
Edit menu - used to assign a name to a person.
Delete menu - used to remove the person icon from the tree.
Menu Music On/Off - used to turn background music on and off.
Menu Redraw - used to change the appearance of the tree.
The educational game is designed for both smartphones and tablets.
