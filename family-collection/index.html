An application for collectors, designed to record and organize collection items.

Program functions:

  • detailed description of the collection item
  • photographs and videos of the collection item
  • copies of documents about the collection item
  • sending information to E-mail, Facebook, Twitter, VKontakte about the subject of the collection
  • Printing reports and photos divided into pages

    • The collection is assembled with jewelry care and love.
