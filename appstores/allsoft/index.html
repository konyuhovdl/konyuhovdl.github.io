MY APPLICATIONS IN MICROSOFT STORES

Family Tree

 "Family Tree" is a program for preserving the history and building a family tree. With its help, you will create a detailed list of family members, add their biographies, photos and videos. The program is easy to learn, has a clear and beautiful interface, while offering powerful tools for creating an expanded family tree. The "Events Calendar" tab allows you to clearly see the days of important family events. You can customize any element of the calendar: fonts, background, month placement and photos.

My boards announcements

 The program is designed for semi-automatic placement of information in catalogs, on electronic boards and in newspapers. The program has a clear and convenient interface.

Encryptor

 Program "Encryptor" is designed to encrypt and decrypt files using the GOST, Blowfish, IDEA, Misty1, Rijndael, Twofish, Cast128, Cast256, RC2, RC5, RC6, DES, TEA password methods.

Animal pedigree

 The program is designed to create a pedigree of animals (horses, dogs, cats, etc.). Maintaining a list of animals, their photos and videos.

Multibase

 The "Multibase" program is designed to work with DBS and DBF. The DBS database is an original development.

Family Events Calendar

 Family Events Calendar - a program for creating beautiful calendars with photos on any year or month. With its help, you can create high-quality photo calendars, just select the format and design of the project, add photos. You can add people with photos to the calendar tied to their birthday. Then the calendar will remind you of memorable dates and birthdays of friends and relatives.
