Preserve history, start with yourself!
An invaluable acquisition for your family is knowledge of your roots. No matter who your ancestors were - nobles or peasants, merchants, townspeople or soldiers. A valuable gift for a person with family values ​​- how your family name was formed, what are its historical and cultural roots.
Not every person knows the name of his great-grandmother or great-grandfather. And the whole point is that his ancestors did not have the opportunity or desire to make a family tree. But you and I will be smarter and we will provide descendants with all the information they need.
And the program "Family Genealogical Tree" will help you with this. You can arrange the information stored in your family archive or the memory of your relatives in the form of a family tree, family album to your taste.
The program will remind you in advance about the upcoming birthday of your family member or relative.

The program is designed to save the history and build a genealogical tree of the family. With its help, you will create a detailed list of family members, add their biographies, photos and video materials. The program is simple and easy to learn, has a clear and beautiful interface.
Program functions:
  •   compiling a biography of each family member;
  •   preserving family history;
  •   constructing a general family tree for all people on the list;
  •   constructing a general family tree along the male or female line;
  •   constructing a personal family tree;
  •   constructing a circular family tree;
  •   constructing a tree of nationalities;
  •   compiling a generational chart;
  •   compiling a general and personal family photo album;
  •   compiling a general and personal videofamily archive;
  •   creating a general and personal file archive;
  •   storing copies of documents;
  •   reminders of the birthday of family members and relatives;
  •   printing a family tree, biographies and photographs of family members;
  •   saving data in an archive;
  •   restoring data from an archive with the function of combining databases;
  •   GEDCOM export/import.

    • Family genealogical tree is a powerful and complex genealogy program. Designed to make it easier for you to collect, display and organize information about your family history. The program allows you to exchange data with relatives through the data archiving function. Family Tree Genealogy provides you with a tool to preserve every bit of information about your family history.
