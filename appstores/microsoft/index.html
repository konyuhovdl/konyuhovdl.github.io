The Family Tree of Family

 The program is designed to preserve family history. Keeps a detailed list of families and their members. Builds a family tree on graphic and hypertext canvas. Creates a book of ancestors. Saves photos and videos. Facilitates the work of collecting, displaying and organizing information about the history and heritage of families.

Genealogical Tree

 The application was created to store the historical heritage of the family. Keeps a list of people and their family ties, photographs, copies of documents and videos. Draws trees on a graphics and hypertext canvas. Saves photos and videos. Shows a calendar of family events, which makes it possible to visually see the days of important family events.

Genealogical Tree 3D

 The study of the pedigree is akin to historical investigation. There are facts, legends, assumptions. Feel like a Researcher of your Family with the Genealogical Tree 3D program.

Pedigree animal

 The program is designed to create a pedigree of animals. Keeps a list of animals, their photos and videos. Shows pet birthday calendar.
